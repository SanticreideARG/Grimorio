[dark fantasy battle music, no lyrics, no vocals, instrumental only]
[D minor, driving tempo 108 BPM, aggressive, urgent]
[loop-ready, seamless loop]

Starts immediately with driving rhythmic ostinato in low strings —
sixteenth notes, relentless. War drums enter at bar 2, heavy downbeats
with fast fills. A brass staccato motif (three sharp notes, descending)
cuts through every four bars like a strike. Strings alternate between
the driving ostinato and short aggressive slashes. A low choir stabs
on beats 1 and 3 — wordless, guttural, just vowels. The piece has one
brief moment of near-silence at the midpoint (one bar), then crashes
back twice as hard. It should feel like tactics under fire: urgent but
controlled, not chaotic. Energy never drops for more than one beat.

Instruments: low string ensemble (driving ostinato), taiko/kettle war drums
(heavy, precise), staccato brass hits, guttural low choir (wordless stabs),
occasional high strings for tension spikes

Mood: tactical urgency, brutal but focused, dark medieval battle,
every decision matters, adrenaline mixed with dread

References: Darkest Dungeon "The Cove" combat theme, Darkest Dungeon 2
battle music, Dragon Age Origins "The Darkspawn Chronicles",
Elden Ring Margit phase 2

NO: heroic/triumphant brass fanfare, modern orchestral epic (Hans Zimmer
trailer style), rock/metal guitars, anything that sounds like a hero
winning — this is survival, not glory
