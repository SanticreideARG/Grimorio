PROMPT PARA SUNO / UDIO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[dark fantasy boss battle music, no lyrics, no vocals, instrumental only]
[B minor, slow-heavy tempo 76 BPM, monumental, desperate, epic dark]
[loop-ready, seamless loop, structured in two sections that loop]

Opens with a massive percussion hit — a single taiko strike that reverberates
for two full bars of near-silence. Then low brass enters in unison: a slow,
grinding descending line like a mountain collapsing. A full low male choir
enters with a two-note motif (minor second interval, dissonant) chanting
wordlessly — not triumphant, but inevitable, like a funeral march for the world.
The rhythm builds: war drums at 76 BPM with heavy syncopation, strings
sawing in short aggressive bursts. A high screaming violin enters at the midpoint,
a cry of desperation over the grinding machinery below. The piece has two
distinct moods that alternate: the grinding "the world ends" brass/choir section,
and a more frantic "last desperate stand" strings/drums section. Both loop.
It should make the player feel they are fighting something enormous and ancient
that does not care whether it wins — because it already has.

Instruments: full low male choir (wordless, dissonant intervals), massive
taiko drums (thunderous), low brass ensemble (trombones, tuba — grinding,
not fanfare), screaming high violin solo, low string cluster chords (sawing,
brutal), distant thunder/rumble sound design underneath

Mood: monumental, inevitable, ancient evil, desperate last stand,
the weight of an entire ruined world in a single fight,
no heroism — only survival

References: Bloodborne "Orphan of Kos", Elden Ring "Starscourge Radahn",
Darkest Dungeon "Swansong" boss theme, Sekiro "Genichiro" phase 2,
Shadow of the Colossus "Counterattack"

NO: triumphant heroic brass, anything in major key, uplifting choir
(no Carmina Burana energy), modern hybrid trailer sound (no 8dio/Two Steps
from Hell style), electric bass or guitar, anything that resolves to tonic
triumphantly — the ending should feel pyrrhic
