PROMPT PARA SUNO / UDIO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

[dark fantasy ambient, no lyrics, no vocals, instrumental only]
[slow tempo 44 BPM, drone-based, atmospheric]
[loop-ready, seamless loop, long form]

A deep sustained string drone in D minor forms the foundation — never
changing pitch, only slowly swelling in dynamic. Over it, an irregular
low percussion pattern (like distant war drums or footsteps on ash)
enters every 6-8 bars unpredictably. A solo bone flute plays a short
melancholic four-note phrase, then silences for many bars before
repeating — slightly varied each time. Sparse pizzicato cello notes
mark the passing of time. Occasional gust of low brass (like a horn
in fog) adds weight. The atmosphere is: walking through a landscape
that used to be beautiful, now devastated. Silence is as important
as sound. No strong melodic hook — this is texture, not song.

Instruments: deep string ensemble drone, bone flute (solo, sparse),
irregular taiko drums (very distant), pizzicato cello, distant horn,
light wind/ambience sound design underneath

Mood: foreboding, melancholy, slow dread, the weight of an ashen world,
something terrible is inevitable

References: Darkest Dungeon hamlet theme, Shadow of the Colossus ambient
sections, Bloodborne "Hunter's Dream"

NO: driving rhythm, energetic melody, bright instruments (flute too high/happy),
modern production, electric instruments, anything that sounds like adventure
or exploration rather than grim survival

NOTAS DE USO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
- Se reproduce en loop durante TODO el recorrido del mapa (la mayor parte
  del tiempo de juego). Es crítico que NO canse tras muchas repeticiones.
- El loop debe ser imperceptible — ideal que empiece y termine en el mismo
  acorde del drone.
- Si suena demasiado "vacío" se puede agregar una segunda variante (mapa2.ogg)
  para los capítulos 3-4 con más tensión.
